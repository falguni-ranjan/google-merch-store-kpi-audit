# Google Merchandise Store — KPI Audit & Website Redesign

An e-commerce analytics and UX/UI redesign project focused on the Google Merchandise Store. This university assignment combines Google Analytics 4 (GA4) data analysis, KPI evaluation, conversion funnel analysis, and a redesigned HTML website prototype.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to evaluate the Google Merchandise Store's digital performance, identify gaps in its current measurement approach, and propose website improvements based on data-driven insights.

The project examines traffic acquisition, user engagement, revenue performance, product interactions, and the purchase journey to identify opportunities for improving the e-commerce experience.

## Objectives

- Audit key performance indicators (KPIs) using GA4 demo data.
- Evaluate traffic acquisition channels and engagement quality.
- Analyse the purchase funnel and identify potential drop-off points.
- Compare mobile, desktop, and tablet performance.
- Review product performance and merchandising opportunities.
- Identify analytics measurement gaps and key risk indicators (KRIs).
- Redesign the website experience to improve navigation, product discovery, and conversion usability.
- Recommend improvements to analytics tracking and performance measurement.

## Project Components

### 1. KPI Audit
Evaluation of key e-commerce metrics, including:
- Sessions and engaged sessions
- Engagement rate
- Average engagement time
- Revenue and purchasers
- Purchase funnel conversion
- Device-level performance
- Traffic attribution quality
- Product performance

### 2. Traffic Acquisition Analysis
Analysis of channel performance to understand how different acquisition sources contribute to website engagement and revenue.

### 3. Conversion Funnel Analysis
Evaluation of the customer journey from initial funnel entry through product views, add-to-cart actions, checkout, and purchase.

### 4. Device Performance Analysis
Comparison of mobile, desktop, and tablet metrics to identify areas that may warrant further usability testing.

### 5. Website Redesign
A responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototype that explores a redesigned shopping experience, with a focus on:
- Navigation and product discovery
- Product catalogue presentation
- Mobile usability
- Shopping cart interactions
- Conversion journey improvements
- Future analytics measurement readiness

### 6. Recommendations
Proposed improvements include:
- Investigating mobile conversion friction.
- Auditing campaign tagging and unassigned traffic.
- Improving purchase funnel event tracking.
- Connecting marketing costs and product margins to business performance analysis.

## Data & Methodology

The analytics findings are based on GA4 screenshots and data supplied for the assignment. The website prototype is a separate demonstration and is not connected to the live Google Merchandise Store analytics, inventory, or checkout systems.

Metrics should be interpreted according to their definitions and available data. Derived calculations, illustrative values, and proposed metrics should be distinguished from directly observed figures.

The analysis does not establish that any particular design change will improve conversion without further testing and post-implementation measurement.

## How to Run the Website

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Locate the website's `index.html` file.
3. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.

If the project uses separate CSS, JavaScript, or asset files, keep the original folder structure intact.

No backend is required for a standalone prototype unless additional functionality is explicitly documented.

## Limitations

- The prototype does not automatically connect to Google's production website or private systems.
- Product information and analytics figures must be verified against the original sources before being treated as authoritative.
- A functioning prototype does not establish a measurable improvement in business performance.
- Profitability cannot be determined without relevant cost and margin data.
